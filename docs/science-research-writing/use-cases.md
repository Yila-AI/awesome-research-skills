# Use Cases

## I have a research idea but no manuscript

**Provide:** question, intended contribution, known design, and any constraints.

**The Skill does:** builds a provisional section-function map and identifies the first evidence needed.

**It will not invent:** a literature gap, hypothesis, method, or result.
**Expected response:** a writing plan, author confirmation if needed, and one next step.

## I have methods and results notes

**Provide:** design, sample, procedures, outcomes, analyses, results, and limitations you already know.

**The Skill does:** inventories supported facts, chooses the first writable section, drafts it, and audits it.

**It will not invent:** missing procedural detail or a plausible mechanism.
**Expected response:** manuscript-ready text plus a short organization explanation.

## I need one manuscript section

**Provide:** the materials supporting that section and its intended message.

**The Skill does:** loads the relevant Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, Abstract, or Title workflow.

**It will not invent:** conventional content just to make the section appear complete.
**Expected response:** the requested section, author queries only where evidence is missing, and the next revision step.

## I have a complete draft

**Provide:** the manuscript and any journal requirements.

**The Skill does:** audits cross-section consistency, result-discussion boundaries, title/abstract promises, citations, limitations, and conclusion reach before revising.

**It will not invent:** a stronger contribution or additional evidence.
**Expected response:** prioritized diagnosis, targeted revisions, and risk flags where needed.

## I have target-journal papers

**Provide:** preferably four or more recent, relevant papers you are permitted to process.

**The Skill does:** identifies recurring information functions, sequence patterns, variation, and exceptions.

**It will not invent or copy:** target-paper sentences, claims, citations, or scientific content.
**Expected response:** a source-aware writing model and a paper-specific section plan.

## My sources conflict

**Provide:** the table, figure, notes, or prose versions that disagree.

**The Skill does:** identifies both values, explains what is blocked, and continues unaffected work.

**It will not do:** silently choose the value that creates a smoother narrative.
**Expected response:** conflict diagnosis and one request for the authoritative source.

## My author intent is in Chinese

Write the intended meaning in Chinese and provide the scientific materials. The Skill can use that intent to organize an English manuscript section while keeping the evidence boundaries explicit. Use `$sci-ssci-polishing` later when the primary task is language translation or polishing of an existing draft.
