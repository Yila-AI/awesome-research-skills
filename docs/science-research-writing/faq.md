# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this an official companion to the book?

No. It is an independent, unofficial open-source Skill inspired by the book's reverse-engineering pedagogy. It is not affiliated with the author or publisher.

## Does the repository contain the book?

No. It contains original workflows, schemas, templates, tests, and documentation. It does not contain the book or reconstructed copyrighted content.

## How is this different from `sci-ssci-polishing`?

Use `science-research-writing` when moving from research materials to a plan, draft, revision, or evidence audit. Use `sci-ssci-polishing` when an existing passage or section primarily needs translation or language polishing.

## Do I need target papers?

No. The Skill starts with a conservative generic empirical-paper model. Target papers improve adaptation to a field and venue when they are legally and practically available.

## Can it write a complete paper from one sentence?

It can plan the paper and identify missing evidence. It will not fabricate the study content needed to complete a manuscript.

## Can it add citations?

Not by invention. Citation search and verification require a separate, explicit evidence workflow. The Skill preserves citations the author supplies and flags unsupported citation requests.

## Does it guarantee publication?

No. It supports writing process and evidence preservation; editorial decisions depend on scientific contribution, validity, fit, reporting, ethics, and peer review.

## Does it work for qualitative or theoretical papers?

The first release targets empirical papers with common Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, Abstract, and Title functions. Qualitative, theoretical, and systematic-review workflows require dedicated extensions.
