# Privacy, Copyright, and Affiliation

## Confidential research material

Use only research materials you are authorized to process. Follow institutional, funder, journal, ethics-board, and collaboration rules. Remove direct identifiers and unnecessary sensitive information. Do not upload confidential peer-review manuscripts or restricted participant data to a service unless its terms and your institution permit it.

## Target papers

Do not commit article PDFs, subscription full text, extracted paragraphs, private access traces, or phrase banks. Public models store bibliographic identifiers, source locators, aggregate structural observations, confidence, and exceptions only.

## Book inspiration

This is an independent, unofficial project inspired by the reverse-engineering pedagogy and section-by-section approach presented in Hilary Glasman-Deal's *Science Research Writing: For Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*.

The repository does not reproduce the book, PDF, scans, exercises, answer key, phrase lists, sample passages, or page content. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author, World Scientific, any journal, or any publisher.

Book-cover artwork or other publisher-owned media is not covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 license and must not be added without documented permission or another verified reuse basis.

## Responsibility

The Skill does not replace the author, statistician, domain expert, professional editor, ethics review, or journal requirements. It does not guarantee correctness, originality, acceptance, or publication.
