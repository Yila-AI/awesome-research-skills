# Methods

## Reader expectation

Enable a qualified reader to understand what was done, to whom or what, in what order, with which measures, and how the evidence was analyzed.

## Required functions

Use only functions supported by the materials:

1. identify design, setting, timing, and preregistration when supplied;
2. describe participants, samples, eligibility, recruitment, or data sources;
3. identify materials, instruments, exposures, interventions, and outcomes;
4. present the procedure in reproducible order;
5. state preprocessing, exclusions, and missing-data handling;
6. describe analyses, software, thresholds, and sensitivity checks;
7. report ethics and consent only when documented.

## Target-paper observations

Observe subsection order, granularity, terminology, passive/active voice conventions, equation placement, software reporting, and where deviations are disclosed. A target paper cannot supply missing procedural facts.

## Section boundaries

Do not justify a method with an invented rationale, report results, claim validation not performed, or add standard-sounding ethics language. Preserve procedural order, instrument names, parameter values, exclusions, and analysis labels exactly.

## Failure modes

- filling missing details with field defaults;
- changing past procedures into recommendations;
- converting planned analyses into completed analyses;
- omitting exclusions or preprocessing that affect interpretation;
- renaming variables or groups inconsistently;
- treating software output as a method description.

## Final audit

Trace every procedural sentence to a supplied source. Verify participants, group labels, instruments, versions, parameters, time points, thresholds, exclusions, statistical tests, ethics statements, and chronology.
