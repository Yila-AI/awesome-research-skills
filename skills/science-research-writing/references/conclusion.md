# Conclusion

## Reader expectation

Leave the reader with the study's supported contribution, not a stronger or broader version of it.

## Required functions

1. synthesize the answer to the research question;
2. state the contribution at the evidence-supported level;
3. preserve the most important scope condition;
4. state an implication or next step only when supported;
5. avoid repeating the Abstract or Discussion word for word.

## Target-paper observations

Observe conclusion length, whether it is a separate section, how claims are bounded, and whether future work is present. Treat absence of a separate conclusion as a valid target pattern.

## Section boundaries

Do not introduce new data, citations, mechanisms, limitations, recommendations, or populations. Do not upgrade feasibility to effectiveness or sample-specific evidence to universal applicability.

## Failure modes

- promotional claims such as `transformative` or `breakthrough`;
- universal scope from a single setting;
- immediate practice recommendations without intervention evidence;
- treating statistical significance as practical importance;
- adding a generic call for more research unrelated to the study's boundary.

## Final audit

Compare the conclusion with the objective, design, primary and null results, limitations, and Abstract. Confirm that every promise is fulfilled and no claim exceeds the Discussion.
