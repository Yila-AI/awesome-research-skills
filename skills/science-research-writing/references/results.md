# Results

## Reader expectation

Answer the analysis questions with a transparent sequence of evidence while preserving magnitude, direction, uncertainty, and null or adverse findings.

## Required functions

1. orient the reader to the analysis or outcome;
2. report descriptive or sample information needed for interpretation;
3. report the primary result with comparison, direction, magnitude, and uncertainty;
4. report secondary, sensitivity, subgroup, null, negative, and adverse results as supplied;
5. refer to tables and figures without duplicating every value;
6. distinguish prespecified from exploratory results when documented.

## Target-paper observations

Observe evidence order, table/figure integration, density of numeric reporting, result-first versus context-first sentences, and treatment of secondary analyses. Do not borrow a paper's interpretation or emphasis.

## Section boundaries

Do not add mechanisms, explanations, implications, recommendations, literature comparison, or causal language unsupported by design. Minimal directional description is not interpretation.

Do not infer direction or meaning from an odds ratio, coefficient, sign, p value, or confidence interval without the supplied variable definition, coding, comparator, and outcome. Do not invent a table or figure callout.

## Failure modes

- reporting only significant outcomes;
- calling a non-significant result a trend without authorization;
- reversing comparator or direction;
- changing denominators, time points, decimals, or uncertainty intervals;
- presenting exploratory analyses as confirmatory;
- repeating the table without an evidence hierarchy.

## Final audit

Match every result to its outcome, comparison, group, time point, estimate, uncertainty, significance, and source. Confirm that null, negative, and adverse findings remain visible and that no new explanation appears.
