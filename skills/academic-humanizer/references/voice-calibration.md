# Author-voice calibration

Use this reference only when the user supplies genuine prior writing or explicitly requests voice matching.

## Choose evidence

Prefer two to five representative samples that are:

- written or approved by the same author;
- from the same language;
- close to the current genre, field, and section type;
- accepted, published, submitted, or otherwise considered representative by the author.

A single short paragraph supports only weak calibration. Co-authored papers may reflect several voices; note that limitation rather than pretending to identify one author perfectly.

## Build a compact style profile

Observe stable choices, not isolated quirks:

- information order within paragraphs;
- typical sentence-length range and clause depth;
- direct versus expository openings;
- active and passive voice balance;
- use of first-person plural;
- frequency and placement of hedging;
- preferred logical connectors;
- how citations are integrated into claims;
- terminology, notation, and abbreviation habits;
- how limitations and implications are introduced;
- section-specific rhetorical moves.

Record the profile privately in no more detail than needed to guide the revision.

## Match functions, not strings

- Reproduce the author's tendencies in rhythm, information order, and rhetorical emphasis.
- Do not copy distinctive sentences, memorable phrases, or unsupported claims from the samples.
- Do not force every sentence into the same pattern.
- Keep the current manuscript's terminology and evidence authoritative when they differ from the samples.
- Treat venue conventions as a second constraint: author voice should remain appropriate for the target publication.

## Confidence labels

Report one of:

- `Strong basis`: multiple representative samples show consistent patterns;
- `Moderate basis`: one substantial or several mixed-author samples provide usable guidance;
- `Limited basis`: the sample is short, mismatched, or heavily co-authored;
- `Not performed`: no author sample was supplied.

Do not report a numerical similarity score unless the workflow actually computed and validated one.

## Privacy and provenance

Use samples only for the requested revision. Do not imply that private manuscripts were uploaded, stored, or used elsewhere. If authorship or permission is uncertain, ask the user to confirm they are authorized to use the sample before extracting or reproducing non-public content.
