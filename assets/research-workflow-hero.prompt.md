## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: GitHub repository README hero cover and social sharing banner, landscape approximately 2:1
Primary request: Create a premium editorial cover for an open-source project that gives AI agents a complete end-to-end academic research workflow.
Scene/backdrop: Deep navy research workspace with subtle paper texture and a restrained technical grid, sophisticated rather than cyberpunk.
Subject: A clear left-to-right research journey connected by one continuous warm teal-and-copper evidence thread: literature discovery represented by a magnifying glass over paper cards; reading and synthesis represented by linked evidence notes; research design and analysis represented by a clean chart and structured notebook; writing and review represented by a manuscript with precise annotations and check marks; communication represented by a polished presentation slide. The stages should feel like one coherent system, not separate floating icons.
Style/medium: High-end editorial illustration with tactile paper, ink, glass, and subtle luminous accents; clean, scholarly, credible, modern open-source tooling aesthetic; suitable for both natural sciences and social sciences.
Composition/framing: Wide panoramic 2:1 composition. Reserve generous uncluttered space in the upper-left/center for two lines of title text. Keep important details inside safe margins so the image remains legible when cropped for GitHub social preview.
Lighting/mood: Confident, calm, rigorous, inviting; warm ivory papers on dark navy, restrained teal and copper highlights.
Color palette: Deep navy, warm ivory, muted teal, copper; high contrast but not neon.
Text (verbatim): "AWESOME RESEARCH SKILLS" and beneath it "THE RESEARCH WORKFLOW EVERY AI AGENT SHOULD HAVE"
Typography: Bold condensed sans-serif for the main title, smaller clean sans-serif subtitle, exact spelling, strong hierarchy, highly legible at thumbnail size.
Constraints: The visual must communicate an expanding full research lifecycle. No number of skills. No SCI/SSCI-only framing. No company logos. No watermarks. No fake citations, tiny unreadable paragraphs, chemical formulas, or discipline-specific scientific claims. Render only the two specified text lines and no other words.
Avoid: generic robot heads, humanoid robots, glowing brains, stock-photo scientists, purple cyberpunk gradients, excessive UI dashboards, clutter, illegible text, extra labels.
```
