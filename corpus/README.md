# Public corpus metadata

This directory contains a reproducibility-oriented metadata release, not a paper full-text dataset.

## Files

- `paper-pool-1000.csv`: 1,000-paper metadata candidate pool.
- `paper-shortlist-200.csv`: balanced screening shortlist.
- `final-corpus-60.csv`: final 60-paper portfolio and frozen split assignment.
- `selection-rubric.md`: selection dimensions and hard exclusions.
- `corpus-summary.md`: aggregate SCI/SSCI and discipline distribution.

## Included fields

The CSV files retain bibliographic facts and project annotations such as title, DOI, publication year, journal, broad discipline cluster, research archetype, and split assignment.

Citation counts are point-in-time metadata and may change. Index-family and quality labels are research-screening annotations, not official certification of an article's quality.

## Excluded material

The public release intentionally excludes:

- article PDFs, XML/JATS full text, and extracted paragraphs;
- institutional-access files and access traces;
- proprietary impact-factor, quartile, and ranking tables;
- library credentials, cookies, tokens, and local file paths.

Titles, DOIs, venue names, and related bibliographic facts originate from public scholarly metadata services and publisher records. External links and third-party facts remain subject to their source terms.
